# Skill: Email Writing
Status: Initial playbook.

## Use for
Promotional emails, onboarding, lifecycle messages, newsletters, follow-ups, and professional correspondence.

## Required inputs
Audience relationship, email purpose, desired action, context, sender identity, tone, relevant offer/details, and any sequence position.

## Process
1. Define one primary purpose and reader action.
2. Match the subject line and opening to the message's actual value.
3. Put the important information early.
4. Use concise paragraphs, natural transitions, and an appropriate tone.
5. Make the CTA clear; for professional correspondence, make requests and deadlines explicit where applicable.
6. Check personalization tokens, links/placeholders, factual claims, and sequence consistency.

## Rules
Do not fake familiarity, imply prior contact that did not happen, invent personal details, or use deceptive subject lines. For sequences, make each email serve a distinct purpose and avoid repetitive messages.
