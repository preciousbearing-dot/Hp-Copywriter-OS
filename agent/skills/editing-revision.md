# Skill: Editing & Revision
Status: Initial playbook.

## Use for
Copy edits, structural edits, tone changes, rewrites, proofreading, and critique.

## First identify the requested level
- Proofread: spelling, grammar, punctuation, obvious typos.
- Copy edit: clarity, consistency, phrasing, and flow while preserving meaning.
- Line edit: sentence-level rhythm, precision, voice, and emphasis.
- Structural edit: organization, argument, hierarchy, and missing content.
- Rewrite: broader changes allowed within the supplied brief.

## Process
1. Confirm what must be preserved and what may change.
2. Diagnose the highest-impact issues first.
3. Edit to the requested level, not beyond it.
4. Preserve factual meaning and mark unclear or contradictory source material.
5. Summarize significant changes when useful.
6. Check that the final version still meets the original purpose and constraints.

## Rules
Do not silently alter legal, technical, numerical, or contractual meaning. When asked to preserve voice, avoid replacing distinctive phrasing with generic polish.
