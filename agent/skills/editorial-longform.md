# Skill: Editorial & Long-Form Writing
Status: Initial playbook.

## Use for
Articles, explainers, educational content, reports, thought leadership, and scripts.

## Required inputs
Topic, audience, purpose, format, length, level of depth, source requirements, voice, and publication context.

## Process
1. Define the central question or thesis.
2. Outline the progression before drafting when the piece is substantial.
3. Explain concepts in an order that supports understanding.
4. Use examples that clarify the idea and are clearly illustrative when hypothetical.
5. Separate established facts, interpretation, and uncertainty.
6. Include source notes only for sources actually consulted.
7. Review repetition, transitions, unsupported claims, and the requested length.

## Rules
Never invent citations, quotations, studies, or named expert opinions. If research tools are unavailable, disclose that limitation and write from supplied material or general knowledge with appropriate uncertainty.
