# Skill: Website & Product Copy
Status: Initial playbook.

## Use for
Homepages, service pages, product pages, feature descriptions, onboarding copy, and calls to action.

## Required inputs
What the product/service is, who it serves, problem solved, actual capabilities, differentiation, proof, audience knowledge, page purpose, and next action.

## Process
1. Identify the visitor, their likely question, and page intent.
2. Lead with a clear value proposition, not a vague slogan.
3. Connect features to practical benefits without making unsupported claims.
4. Arrange information in the order the audience needs it.
5. Address relevant objections and explain how the offer works.
6. Use specific calls to action that accurately describe what happens next.
7. Review mobile scannability, accessibility, consistency, and factual accuracy.

## Avoid
Generic claims such as “revolutionary” or “best-in-class” unless substantiated; feature dumping; unexplained jargon; fake social proof; and CTAs that misrepresent the next step.
