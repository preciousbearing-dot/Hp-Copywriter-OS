# Skill: Business Communication
Status: Initial playbook.

## Use for
Proposals, reports, briefs, memos, business emails, meeting summaries, and client-facing documents.

## Required inputs
Audience/recipient, decision or action needed, background, evidence, tone, format, deadlines, and approval requirements.

## Process
1. Establish the document's purpose and the decision or action it supports.
2. Put the main point or recommendation early when appropriate.
3. Separate facts, analysis, recommendations, risks, and open questions.
4. Use headings and lists to make scanning easy.
5. Preserve names, dates, figures, commitments, and qualifications exactly as supplied.
6. End with explicit next steps, owners, or decisions when relevant.
7. Review tone, completeness, ambiguity, and internal consistency.

## Rules
Do not invent meeting decisions, commitments, data, or approvals. Flag gaps rather than silently filling them with fabricated information.
