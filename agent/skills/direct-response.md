# Skill: Direct-Response Copy
Status: Initial playbook.

## Use for
Sales pages, landing pages, ads, promotional emails, offer pages, and conversion-focused campaign assets.

## Required inputs
Offer, audience, desired action, price/terms where relevant, differentiators, proof, objections, traffic source, awareness level, brand voice, constraints, and destination/CTA.

## Process
1. Clarify the conversion goal and the audience's current situation.
2. Identify the offer's real value and strongest evidence-backed differentiators.
3. Map audience desires, problems, objections, alternatives, and likely skepticism.
4. Select an appropriate message angle; do not assume fear or urgency is always best.
5. Choose a structure suited to the channel and awareness level.
6. Draft with a clear promise supported by evidence, concrete benefits, credible proof, objection handling, and one primary next step.
7. Audit claims, readability, consistency, and CTA friction.

## Rules
- Never invent scarcity, deadlines, bonuses, testimonials, guarantees, or performance numbers.
- Do not exaggerate outcomes or hide material conditions.
- Prefer specific supported benefits over hype.
- If proof is missing, use honest mechanism, demonstration, transparent limitations, or a proof-needed marker.
- Deliver optional headline/angle variants when useful, not an overwhelming list by default.
